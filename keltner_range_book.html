The ungated book makes its money in 2021 and 2025 and gives it back in 2022, 2024 and 2026. The gate removes the giving-back without removing the making.

The specification

Rules, in execution order

  1. Universe

    Every USD product with at least 500 daily bars of history. Currently 263. Breadth is load-bearing: restricted to the 28 oldest products the edge drops to t = 1.25 and stops being significant.

  2. Regime gate — checked first, every day

    Build an equal-weight index of the universe. Trade only when it closes above its own 200-day mean, lagged one bar. When it is below, the book opens nothing. This keeps 25% of signals and removes every losing year.

  3. Per-symbol condition

    Kaufman efficiency ratio over 100 bars below 0.30 — the instrument is churning rather than travelling.

  4. Entry trigger

    Close crosses below the lower Keltner band, set at EMA20 − 2 × ATR14. Enter at that close.

  5. Target and stop

    +0.5 ATR target, −3 ATR stop. Small target, wide stop — the inverse of the mid-band target assumed for weeks, and the inversion is what made it work.

  6. Time stop

    Close at market after 16 days if neither level is reached.

  7. Sizing and concurrency

    40 slots; each position takes equity / 40 of margin at 3×. Signals arriving when all slots are full are dropped, never queued. One position per product.

  8. Costs applied throughout

    2bp maker in, 2bp maker out on the target, 5bp taker plus 10bp slippage on the stop.

What would change these numbers

  • The gate's 200-day parameter was chosen on the whole sample. It needs walking forward before the no-losing-years claim is fully earned. The 200-day is the most standard setting there is, which limits but does not eliminate the concern.
  • Delisted products are invisible. Bounded rather than solved: the edge is the same or larger in products that lost 90%+ of their value, and the median product in the universe is down 92%, so this is not a winners' club. It is still not the dead.
  • The deflated-Sharpe hurdle has not been applied across everything tested today, and a great deal was tested.
  • Leverage above 3× is not quotable until the liquidation model is rebuilt — see the flag below.
  • Ten trades a month against your target of fifty. The gate is off three days in four, so the only honest lever is more instruments, not faster trading. 300 products are now ingested against the 263 used here.
Simulator bug, found and logged

Losses are capped at posted margin, so above roughly 5× every loss saturates while gains compound unbounded. This made 10× show a better drawdown than 5×, which is impossible. All results above 3× are void until it is fixed.
