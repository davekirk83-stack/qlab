Where each element actually works

104 elements measured at seven timeframes on 49 crypto symbols, horizon fixed at 20 bars so it scales with the bar. Ranked on net information ratio — IC × √breadth, minus a 6bp round-trip cost drag — because raw IC flatters slow timeframes and the cross-symbol t-stat flatters fast ones.

The cost floor

Gross information is flat. Cost is what separates the timeframes.

gross IR cost drag bars scaled to the largest value across all seven

At 15 minutes the cost of trading exceeds all the information available, before any element is chosen. That is why every intraday book has failed, and it is a floor rather than a haircut.

Read the symbol counts. The figure under each cell is how many symbols carried the same sign. Daily rows mostly rest on 16–18 symbols, not 49, because a 200-period element needs 200 daily bars and most of the panel is too short. The 2h and 8h rows carry 37–43. A daily result with 16/18 behind it is weaker evidence than an 8h result with 42/49, whatever the net IR says.

Levels are overstated, the comparison is not. 49 crypto symbols are not 49 independent bets — they move together, which inflates breadth and therefore every IR here. That bias is identical at every timeframe, so which timeframe wins is trustworthy; the absolute numbers are not achievable Sharpe.
