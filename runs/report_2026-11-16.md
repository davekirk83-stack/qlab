## TWO DEFECTS FOUND — need DK's decision

### 1. Book 7 (Ignition) erases its own record on a halt day — CAUGHT AND REVERTED

paperbook7.py replays the whole book from its start date every run, and its halt
check sits INSIDE that replay loop (line 124: `if flatten == 1 ... continue`).
With a halt active, every historical entry is suppressed, so equity is rebuilt as
START + nothing: the run printed real $2,000.00 / 0 closed and wrote that over
runs/paper7_state.json, replacing the book's whole forward record since 19 Oct
($3,736.60, 56 closed trips, shadow $1,902.86 / 280).

Caught before the commit. paper7_state.json restored from git; the false
paper7_2026-11-16.sql was quarantined and NOT applied to D1, so pb7_equity still
ends at 15 Nov with the true figures. Book 7 was therefore NOT advanced today and
has no 16 Nov row. It had nothing to do in any case: no open position, and its
data ends Friday 13 Nov (already booked through the 15th).

The fix is a one-line scope change — the halt should block only entries at or
after the un-booked tail, not the whole replay — but amending a book's engine is
not this task's authority, so nothing was changed. Until it is fixed, EVERY halt
day will blank book 7, and the next run will do it again unless book 7 is skipped.
